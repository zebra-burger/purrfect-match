Privacy

Purrfect Match · last updated 19 October 2026

Purrfect Match is a free cat-themed puzzle game made as a personal gift. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your game progress (levels, stars, boosters and play statistics) is saved in your browser on your own device. The game works fully offline.

Signing in with Google (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, the game receives your Google account's identifier, email address and name. They're used only to keep a cloud backup of your game progress so it can come back on a new phone. Nothing else from your Google account is accessed.

What the cloud backup stores

  • Your game progress, as described above.
  • Your Google account identifier, email address and name, linked to that backup.
  • A sign-in session for each phone you use (stored in scrambled form).

This is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts the game. It is never sold, shared, used for advertising or used for anything other than the backup.

Removing your data

Signing out on the journey map ends that phone's session. To have your cloud backup deleted entirely, ask the person who shared this game with you and it will be removed.
